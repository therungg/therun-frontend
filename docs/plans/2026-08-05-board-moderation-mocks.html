Moderation happens on the board

Seven states from the board-moderation design. Every verb acts from the leaderboard itself, every action carries a reason, and every reason is public. These are static mocks at product fidelity — checkboxes toggle and the bulk bar reacts, nothing else is wired.

Fig. 3

Preview, reason, apply

Every destructive verb goes through the same three beats, driven by the existing /preview endpoints. The preview is what the mod is agreeing to — counts first, then who is affected. The reason is required, and it is the text that will appear in the public log, so the field says so.

  • Counts before names — four numbers carry the blast radius; the breakdown table is for checking it.
  • Consequences are called out, not left to be discovered: a rank-1 change gets its own line.
  • The apply button restates the scope (“Remove 4 runs”), never a bare “Confirm”.
  • Reversibility is stated in the footer, because it is the reason removal can be routine.

  • Toggle “Moderator view” in the frame — the two redacted entries switch to the real identity.
  • Verb pills carry the semantic colour: green for verified/restored, red for removed/banned, neutral for repairs.
  • Reason is the body, quoted and full-width — it is the point of the log, not metadata.
  • Every row links out: to the run's page (removed runs land on their tombstone) or the runner panel.
Interpretation. The spec's example — “a runner was banned from this game: [reason]” — redacts identity for a plain ban, but the transparency decision says removals are always attributable and profiles carry no ban badge. These mocks redact only anonymized runners and show the ban target by name in the mod view; if bans should be publicly anonymous by default, the public string in the third entry is the one to change.

Anonymous runners are still one runner

Redaction swaps a stable placeholder in at read time, keyed per user and game. The runs stay where they are; the identity is gone. Because the placeholder is stable, three runs by the same hidden runner still group as one runner — nobody farms extra board slots by going anonymous.

What a visitor cannot do
No profile link, no avatar, no country flag, no VOD attribution. The row is muted but keeps its rank, its time and its history.
Mod view tatallgrass — mods always resolve the real identity; they need it to enforce.
  • Dashed monogram stands in for the avatar so the runner column keeps its rhythm — no ragged left edge.
  • Name is not italic and not greyed to unreadable; it is a name, just not theirs.
  • Permanent by design — the lift path exists but is admin-only.
  • Every public surface resolves through the same step: board, run page, history, log, recent PBs, standings, search.

Static mocks. Checkbox selection and the moderator-view toggle are live; everything else is inert. Colour, spacing, table anatomy and panel surfaces are taken from the live games-v2 board (_board.scss, _design-tokens.scss, _overrides.scss) so nothing here needs a new visual primitive — the new pieces are a checkbox column, a bulk bar, one dialog shape, a log list and two page states.
